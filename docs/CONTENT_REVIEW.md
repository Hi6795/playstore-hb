# Content review

Submitter supplies identity/contact/source/homepage, exact package/media, controls, licenses, redistribution evidence, attribution, changelog, model/firmware results, known issues, and original-file requirements. Automated checks validate structure, bytes/hash, identifiers, MIME by content, dimensions, paths, URLs, completeness, duplicates, and warnings; they do not decide legality.

An independent Reviewer confirms the real project, actual PS4 launch, package/project match, rights evidence, license texts, authentic screenshots, absence of improperly included commercial data, precise compatibility claims, and reviewed hash. A Publisher alone builds/signs/promotes. A Submitter cannot approve their own submission. Revoked legal status blocks updates and next publication.
